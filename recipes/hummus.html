Hummus

Description

The Ultimate Creamy Hummus Experience the secret to incredibly light and fluffy hummus with this foolproof recipe. The key lies in the technique: blending tahini with ice-cold water and lemon juice before adding the chickpeas. This creates a pale, airy base that results in a velvety-smooth texture, far superior to store-bought versions.

Authentic Flavor & Perfect Texture Seasoned with aromatic cumin, fresh garlic, and high-quality olive oil, this hummus is a masterclass in balance. Whether you serve it as a dip or a creamy spread, the long blending time ensures a professional, silken finish. Simply top with a drizzle of olive oil for an authentic Mediterranean touch that’s ready to impress.

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas (~265 g drained weight)
  • 100 ml water (ice-cold!)
  • 1-2 garlic cloves
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 120 g creamy, runny tahini (unsalted)
  • 1-2 tbsp olive oil
Steps
  1. Drain the chickpeas, reserving 50 ml of the chickpea water (aquafaba). Set the chickpeas aside.
  2. Place the lemon juice, the reserved chickpea water, the garlic, and ½ tsp salt into the blender and blend until smooth.
  3. Add the tahini to the blender and mix thoroughly.
    4. Slowly pour the ice-cold water into the blender while it is running. The mixture should become pale and fluffy.
  4. Add the chickpeas, 1–2 tbsp olive oil, and ½ tsp cumin. Blend for at least 3 minutes until the texture is creamy and velvety.
  5. Season to taste with additional salt and lemon juice. If the hummus is too thick, blend in a little more cold water.
  6. Drizzle with olive oil before serving.
